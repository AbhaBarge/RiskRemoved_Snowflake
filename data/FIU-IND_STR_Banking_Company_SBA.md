# FIU-IND Suspicious Transaction Report (STR) for a Banking Company

- **Issuer:** Financial Intelligence Unit – India (FIU-IND)
- **Form code:** SBA01 to SBA04 (one code per page; 4 pages + instructions)
- **Legal basis:** Prevention of Money Laundering Act (PMLA) 2002. Every banking company must furnish details of suspicious transactions, whether or not made in cash.
- **Fill-in convention:** The original form is filled in CAPITAL letters. Read the instructions before filling. Sections marked "DO NOT FILL. FOR FIU-IND USE ONLY" are reserved for FIU-IND.
- **Source note:** This file is a text conversion of the PDF. Checkbox and date-box layouts are described in words. Annexures A, B and C (referenced below) are NOT included in the source PDF.

## 3. Instructions

### General Instructions

**The Prevention of Money Laundering Act 2002.** Under PMLA 2002, every banking company shall furnish details of suspicious transactions, whether or not made in cash.

**How to submit.**
- Every bank branch must submit this form to the Director, FIU-IND, only through the principal officer of the banking company designated under PMLA 2002.
- In urgent cases, the form should also be sent by fax.
- **All annexures must be enclosed.**

**Address:**

```
Director, FIU-IND
Financial Intelligence Unit-India
6th Floor, Hotel Samrat
Chanakyapuri, New Delhi - 110021
India
Fax: +91-11-26874459
```

### Explanation of Specific Terms

**Suspicious transaction** means a transaction, whether or not made in cash, which, to a person acting in good faith:

- (a) gives rise to a reasonable ground of suspicion that it may involve the proceeds of crime; or
- (b) appears to be made in circumstances of unusual or unjustified complexity; or
- (c) appears to have no economic rationale or bonafide purpose.

### Part-wise Instructions

**PART 1: Details of Report**
- **1.1** Date of sending report is the date on which the principal officer sends the report to the Director (FIU-IND).
- **1.2** A replacement report is a report submitted in replacement of an earlier STR. When a replacement report is submitted, the date of submitting the original STR may be mentioned, and the **complete STR has to be submitted again**.

**PART 2: Details of Principal Officer**
- **2.3** ID allotted by FIU-IND may be left blank until it is communicated by FIU-IND.
- **2.4** Category of the bank (codes below).
- **2.5** Principal officer is the officer designated by the banking company under PMLA 2002.

## 4. Reasons for Suspicion: Examples of Suspicious Transactions

These map to the tick-boxes in Part 7.1.

| Code | Reason category | Examples of suspicious transaction |
|------|-----------------|-------------------------------------|
| A | Identity of client | False identification documents; identification documents which could not be verified within reasonable time; accounts opened with names very close to other established business entities |
| B | Background of client | Suspicious background or links with known criminals |
| C | Multiple accounts | Large number of accounts having a common account holder, introducer or authorized signatory with no rationale; unexplained transfers between multiple accounts with no rationale |
| D | Activity in accounts | Unusual activity compared with past transactions; sudden activity in dormant accounts; activity inconsistent with what would be expected from declared business |
| E | Nature of transactions | Unusual or unjustified complexity; no economic rationale or bonafide purpose; frequent purchases of drafts or other negotiable instruments with cash; nature of transactions inconsistent with what would be expected from declared business |
| F | Value of transactions | Value just under the reporting threshold amount in an apparent attempt to avoid reporting; value inconsistent with the client's apparent financial standing |
| Z | Other | Any other reason (specify) |

## 5. Filing Checklist (derived from the form)

1. Complete Parts 1–3 (report, principal officer, reporting branch).
2. List every linked individual (Part 4), entity (Part 5) and account (Part 6). Attach Annexure A, B or C for each one and tick the confirm box.
3. Tick the reasons for suspicion (7.1). Write the summary and sequence of events (7.2 and 7.3).
4. State any investigation and the agency contact details (8.1).
5. Record the number of extra sheets for Parts 4–8, if any.
6. The principal officer signs and dates the form. The name must match Part 2.
7. Send it to the Director, FIU-IND, through the principal officer. Fax it as well if urgent.
8. If replacing an earlier STR, tick YES in 1.2, give the original date in 1.3, and resubmit the complete STR.
